About ISC FEB Untan

International Student Committee (ISC) FEB Untan is a student organization for International Class students in Management and Accounting at the Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Tanjungpura. We serve as a platform for students to connect, grow, and explore self development opportunities.

Through academic, organizational, and meaningful programs, ISC supports students in developing their potential, building meaningful connections, and gaining experiences beyond the classroom. Act Locally, Think Internationally.

ABOUT US

A Space to Learn, Connect, and Grow Together

ISC provides a space for International Class students to learn, connect, and take part in activities beyond the classroom. Through academic activities, student programs, and shared opportunities, we encourage students to develop themselves and contribute to the community while building lasting connections through meaningful experiences together.
